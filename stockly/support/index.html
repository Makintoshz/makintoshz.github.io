FAQ

How do I add products with a barcode?

Tap the scan button, point the camera at the barcode, then enter the name and quantity. Allow camera access when asked.

How do I set low-stock alerts?

Open a product and set its minimum quantity. You'll get a notification when stock drops below it. Make sure notifications are allowed in your phone settings.

How do I back up my data?

On iPhone, enable iCloud for Stockly. On Android, turn on Google Drive backup in the app settings. We can't recover data for you, so keep backup on.

Can I move my data from iPhone to Android?

Yes. Export your inventory to a file from the app settings, then import it on the other device.
