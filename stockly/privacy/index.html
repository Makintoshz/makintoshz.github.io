Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Summary

Stockly does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your inventory data belongs to you.

Where your data is stored

Products, quantities and reports are stored only on your device and, if you enable backup, in your own cloud account (iCloud on iOS, Google Drive on Android). We have no servers and no access to your data.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes on your device. No images are saved or sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-up
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No ads
  • No sharing of data with third parties
