Summary

NoteApp does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your notes belong to you.

Where your notes are stored

Your notes are stored only on your device and, if you enable it, in your own private iCloud account. We have no servers and no access to your notes.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-up
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No ads
  • No sharing of data with third parties

Face ID

Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. NoteApp never sees or stores your face data.
