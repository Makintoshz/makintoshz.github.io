FAQ

How do I sync notes between iPhone and iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple ID on both devices and make sure iCloud is enabled for NoteApp in Settings. Sync happens automatically.

How do I lock a note with Face ID?

Open the note, tap the menu, and choose Lock. Next time you open it, Face ID will be required.

Can you see or recover my notes?

No. Your notes live only on your device and your own iCloud, so we can't access them. Keep iCloud enabled to have a backup.

Does NoteApp support Arabic?

Yes, fully, including right-to-left writing.
